The MORVA Standard

The principles behind the work.

This is how MORVA approaches every website: the standards we hold ourselves to before anything is shown to you.

  1. 01

    Clarity before complexity

    Every page starts with one question: what does a visitor need to understand, and how quickly? We remove whatever gets in the way.

  2. 02

    Design with a purpose

    Layout, type and colour are chosen to support a business goal, not to decorate. If an element doesn’t help, it goes.

  3. 03

    Thoughtful details

    Spacing, alignment, states and small pieces of text get the same care as the headline. Small things make a site feel trustworthy.

  4. 04

    Responsive by design

    Every layout is composed for phones, tablets and desktops from the start, not squeezed in at the end.

  5. 05

    Performance and usability

    Quick to load, simple to navigate, comfortable to read. A beautiful site that frustrates people isn’t doing its job.

  6. 06

    Consistency, start to finish

    One visual language from the first impression to the last interaction, so the whole experience feels like a single considered piece.
